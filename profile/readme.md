## What it does

Bills Must Be Paid is a darkly comedic life simulator where you juggle jobs, relationships, and ever-mounting expenses. The struggle is the point. The stress is the joke.

This trainer removes the stress. **Unlimited money** pays every bill, **instant job completion** skips the work mini-games, and **max relationships** unlocks all social content. **Unlock all careers** gives you full career freedom from the start.
